# VoidCasino

A modular, dark-obsidian and neon-purple casino hub addon built for **WoW: Forever** (Interface `16001`). Features a unified simulated credit bankroll, a 1-hour empty-balance refill lockout, real-time guild leaderboards, and pure-code UI rendering with custom suit sprites.

## Disclaimer & AI Disclosure
* **Built Just for Fun:** This addon is a personal hobby project created purely for entertainment with friends and guildmates. All credits, wagers, and payouts are 100% simulated—no real money or in-game gold is ever wagered or exchanged.
* **AI-Generated Codebase:** This project is entirely coded using AI (Gemini) through iterative prompting, testing, and debugging.

## Included & Planned Modules
* **Hub:** Unified bankroll tracker, 1-hour auto-refill timer, cross-game round log, and a pop-out Guild Leaderboard drawer (Top 5 Live Balances, Top 5 All-Time Peak Credits, and per-game high scores).
* **Crash:** 60 FPS curve renderer with dynamic segment scaling, auto-cashout, auto-bet, and verifiable round hashes.
* **Blackjack:** 6-deck shoe, 3:2 natural payouts, dealer stands on all 17s (S17), Hit, Stand, Double Down, and Split.
* **Roulette:** *(In Development)*
* **Video Poker:** *(In Development)*
* **Hi-Lo:** 0–100 interactive slider with Roll Over / Roll Under toggle, live win chance & payout multiplier (1% house edge), quick presets, bet controls, and roll history.

## Slash Commands
* `/vcasino` or `/voidcasino` — Toggle the VoidCasino window.
